# Dokandar Inventory - Complete Flutter Inventory Management System

![Dokandar Inventory Preview](https://github.com/mdrakibulhaquesardar/Dokandar-Inventory-App/blob/CodeCanyon_v1/iDocs/assets/inlinenew.png?raw=true)

**Dokandar Inventory** is a professional, feature-rich inventory management application built with Flutter. Perfect for small to medium businesses, retail stores, and shop owners who need a comprehensive solution to manage products, track sales, handle customers, and generate invoices - all offline without requiring internet connectivity.

## Key Features

### Inventory Management
- Product management with SKU tracking
- Category organization system
- Real-time stock quantity tracking
- Low stock alerts and notifications
- Stock history tracking

### Sales Counter & POS
- Real-time cart management
- Quick product search and add
- Stock validation before checkout
- Discount and tax calculations
- Multiple payment methods support
- Due payment tracking

### Invoice System
- Professional PDF invoice generation
- Full-screen invoice preview
- Print invoices directly
- Share invoices via email, WhatsApp, etc.
- Automatic invoice numbering

### Customer & Supplier Management
- Complete customer database with contact information
- Due payment tracking per customer
- Payment history
- Supplier database management
- Purchase tracking

### Reports & Analytics
- Sales reports (daily, weekly, monthly, custom date range)
- Transaction history (sales and expenses)
- Revenue analytics
- Dashboard with key statistics

### Additional Features
- Employee management with salary tracking
- Expense tracking with categories
- Local backup/restore (JSON export/import)
- Multi-language support (Bengali & English)
- Light, Dark & System theme
- Store configuration and setup wizard

## Technical Specifications

**Framework:** Flutter 3.5.4+ / Dart 3.5.4+

**Architecture:**
- MVC (Model-View-Controller) pattern
- GetX 4.7.2 for state management
- Isar Database 3.1.0 (local NoSQL)
- Offline-first architecture

**Platform Support:**
- Android 5.0+ (API 21)
- Target SDK: API 34 (Android 14)

**Key Dependencies:**
- get (state management, routing)
- isar (local database)
- pdf (invoice generation)
- syncfusion_flutter_pdfviewer (PDF viewing)
- printing (print functionality)
- share_plus (sharing)
- intl (localization)
